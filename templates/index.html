How to use:
  1. Allow microphone permission if asked.
  2. Click Start Listening, then ask a complete question. You do not need a wake word.
  3. Wait for NAVIS to answer aloud; it will then listen for your next question.
  4. While NAVIS is speaking, say “Stop” or “Stop NAVIS” to interrupt it. For hazard checks, click Enable Camera once to grant browser permission. Then say “Hazard” or “Start Hazard Monitoring” to begin, “Stop Hazard Monitoring” to stop, or “Describe Hazards” for a one-time check; you can also use the camera buttons.
Camera permission must be granted with a button click; browsers do not allow a spoken command to grant camera access. Hazard monitoring checks frames periodically and gives image-based warnings. Apparent size or growth is only a visual cue, not a distance measurement. Keep using a white cane or guide dog; do not rely on NAVIS to verify a safe path.
